Help centre

Answers to common questions.

Common questions about installation, reading, audio and more.

How do I install the APK?

Use only an official APK source. Open the downloaded APK and follow Android's prompts. Review any security warning carefully and avoid files from unknown sources.

How do I update the application?

Use Google Play if the official listing is available. Otherwise, install only a verified newer APK from the developer. A newer version may be needed to update an existing installation.

Does Quran reading work offline?

The app is intended to support offline reading. Depending on the release, required content may need to be present on the device first. Test offline mode after initial setup.

How do audio downloads and caching work?

Audio availability and caching behavior depend on the current build. Check the in-app audio controls and verify a recitation plays after disconnecting from the internet before relying on offline playback.

How is Bangla pronunciation shown?

Bangla pronunciation means a Bangla-script guide to Arabic sounds; it is not a Bangla translation. Pronunciation guides cannot replace instruction from a qualified Quran teacher.

Are bookmarks and reading progress available?

Yes. The app includes bookmarks and reading progress so you can continue where you left off.

Does it check Tajweed or recitation with AI?

An automated Tajweed analyzer or AI recitation checker is not confirmed as a released feature. Do not rely on the app for automated correction unless a future release explicitly documents it.

What should I do if the app has a problem?

Restart the app and device, check available storage, and make sure the app is up to date. When reporting a problem, include your Android version, app version, steps to reproduce and a screenshot if possible. Do not send passwords or private information.

How can I contact support?

Email support@hafezyquran.xyz with a concise description of your question or issue.
